Women's Day(国际妇女节)

International Women's Day performances

On March 7th, our university organized a special event at the Ganzhou City Auditorium to celebrate International Women's Day. The event featured a variety of inspiring activities aimed at empowering women and recognizing their contributions. Highlights included interactive workshops on women's health and career development, and cultural performances by talented local women. One of the most memorable moments was our dance performance to the Ganzhou song, which captivated the audience and celebrated our cultural heritage. Additionally, attendees engaged in networking sessions and discussions focused on gender equality and women's rights. The atmosphere was vibrant and uplifting, fostering a sense of unity and shared purpose among participants. This event not only celebrated the achievements of women but also underscored our university's commitment to promoting an inclusive and supportive environment for all.

3月7日，我校在赣州市礼堂举办了庆祝国际妇女节的特别活动。该活动以各种鼓舞人心的活动为特色，旨在增强妇女权能并表彰她们的贡献。亮点包括关于妇女健康和职业发展的互动讲习班，以及当地有才华的妇女的文化表演。最难忘的时刻之一是我们随着赣州歌曲的舞蹈表演，它吸引了观众，庆祝了我们的文化遗产。此外，与会者还参加了以两性平等和妇女权利为重点的网络会议和讨论。气氛充满活力，令人振奋，促进了与会者的团结感和共同目标。这次活动不仅庆祝了妇女的成就，还强调了我们大学致力于促进一个包容和支持所有人的环境。

women's Day

On March 7th, our university hosted a vibrant event at the Ganzhou City Auditorium to celebrate International Women's Day. The day was packed with diverse activities, including a showcase of local art and crafts created by talented women in our community. A particularly special moment was when we gathered with friends to capture memories, reflecting the spirit of camaraderie and support that the day embodied. Various booths offered information on women's rights, health resources, and career opportunities, making the event both educational and empowering. The celebration underscored the importance of unity and the collective effort toward gender equality.

3月7日，我校在赣州市礼堂举办了一场充满活力的国际妇女节庆祝活动。当天有各种各样的活动，包括展示我们社区有才华的女性创作的当地艺术和手工艺。一个特别的时刻是，我们与朋友们聚在一起捕捉回忆，反映了这一天所体现的同志情谊和支持精神。各种展位提供了有关妇女权利、卫生资源和职业机会的信息，使此次活动既具有教育意义，又具有赋权意义。庆祝活动强调了团结和集体努力实现性别平等的重要性。
