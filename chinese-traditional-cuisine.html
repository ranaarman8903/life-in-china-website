Traditional Cuisine(传统美食)

Fried rice (炒饭菜)

Fried rice, known as 炒饭 (chǎo fàn), is my most favorite food in China. When I landed in China, the first dish I tried was fried rice, and I loved it right away. Almost every week, I enjoy the fried rice of Lanzhou, which comes with a delicious soup that complements it perfectly. Fried rice is made by stir-frying cooked rice with vegetables, eggs, and sometimes meat or seafood, seasoned with soy sauce and other spices. The combination of flavors and textures makes it a delightful and satisfying meal.

炒饭，被称为炒饭，是我在中国最喜欢的食物。当我抵达中国时，我尝试的第一道菜是炒饭，我马上就喜欢上了。几乎每周，我都喜欢兰州的炒饭，配上美味的汤，与之相得益彰。炒饭是将煮熟的米饭与蔬菜、鸡蛋，有时还有肉或海鲜一起翻炒，用酱油和其他香料调味制成的。口味和质地的结合使它成为一顿令人愉快和满足的饭。

Hot Pot Cuisine (火锅美食)

Hot pot cuisine was a completely new experience for me when I first tried it in China, and it quickly became one of my favorites. The spicy hot pot, in particular, was so delicious that I found myself craving it regularly. Hot pot is a beloved dish in China, where diners cook a variety of ingredients like thinly sliced meats, vegetables, tofu, and noodles in a simmering pot of flavorful broth. This communal and interactive dining experience is enjoyed by everyone, making it a staple in Chinese culinary culture.

当我第一次在中国尝试火锅时，它对我来说是一种全新的体验，很快就成为了我的最爱之一。尤其是麻辣火锅，非常美味，我发现自己经常想吃它。火锅在中国是一道受人喜爱的菜肴，食客们在炖好的一锅美味肉汤中烹饪各种食材，如切成薄片的肉、蔬菜、豆腐和面条。每个人都喜欢这种公共互动的用餐体验，使其成为中国烹饪文化的主要内容。

Beef Noodles(牛肉面)

Beef noodles are another favorite of mine in China. As everyone knows, both Chinese locals and foreigners love noodles here. China is famous for its wide variety of noodles, and beef noodles are particularly popular. The dish consists of tender beef slices and hot noodles served in a flavorful soup, often garnished with greens and herbs. The combination of the rich broth, succulent beef, and chewy noodles makes it a comforting and delicious meal. While it may not be my absolute favorite, I really like beef noodles for their hearty and satisfying taste.

牛肉面是我在中国的另一个最爱。众所周知，中国本地人和外国人都喜欢这里的面条。中国以面条种类繁多而闻名，牛肉面尤其受欢迎。这道菜由嫩牛肉片和热面条组成，配以美味的汤，通常点缀着绿色蔬菜和香草。浓郁的肉汤、多汁的牛肉和有嚼劲的面条相结合，使它成为一顿舒适美味的大餐。虽然它可能不是我绝对的最爱，但我真的很喜欢牛肉面，因为它的味道鲜美而令人满意。
