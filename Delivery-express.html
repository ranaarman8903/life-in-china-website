Delivery Express(快递)

University Delivery Express

At Gannan Normal University, the delivery express system is a unique and efficient service, reflecting China's advanced logistics network. Within the campus, there is a designated area where all deliveries are received. Students and staff can collect their packages by following a simple procedure. When a delivery arrives, the seller provides a specific number associated with the package. Recipients go to the delivery area, enter this number into a computer system, and the corresponding location of their package is displayed. This streamlined process ensures that packages are organized and easily accessible, minimizing wait times and enhancing convenience. The delivery express system at Gannan Normal University exemplifies the efficiency and innovation of China’s logistics infrastructure.
