Traditional Cuisine(传统美食)

Biryani

Biryani, one of my favorite dishes and a favorite in Pakistan, is a tasty rice dish with marinated meat, spices, and basmati rice. To make biryani, marinate the meat with yogurt and spices. Fry onions until golden, then cook the meat until browned. Add tomatoes and cook until soft. Parboil the rice with whole spices, layer it over the meat, and add saffron milk, fried onions, and herbs. Cover and cook on low heat for 20-30 minutes. Serve hot, topped with fried onions and fresh herbs. Biryani's rich flavors and variety make it truly special.

(Biryani)是我最喜欢的菜之一，也是巴基斯坦的最爱，它是一道美味的米饭，里面有腌制的肉、香料和印度香米。制作(Biryani)，用酸奶和香料腌制肉。把洋葱炒至金黄色，然后把肉煮至褐色。加入西红柿煮软。把米饭和所有的香料一起煮开，把它铺在肉上，加入藏红花牛奶、炸洋葱和香草。盖上盖子，用小火煮20-30分钟。趁热食用，淋上炸洋葱和新鲜香草。(Biryani)丰富的口味和多样性使其真正与众不同。

Mutton Karahi

Mutton Karahi, a famous dish in Pakistan, is a delicious and traditional curry known for its rich flavors and aromatic spices. This popular cuisine is made by cooking tender mutton pieces in a wok-like pot called a karahi with tomatoes, green chilies, garlic, ginger, and a blend of spices. The dish is renowned for its simplicity and quick preparation, using fresh ingredients that enhance its taste. Often enjoyed with naan or rice, Mutton Karahi's bold and savory flavors make it a beloved favorite, showcasing the vibrant and diverse culinary heritage of Pakistan.

羊肉（Karahi）是巴基斯坦的一道名菜，是一种美味的传统咖喱，以其丰富的风味和芳香的香料而闻名。这道受欢迎的美食是在一个叫做karahi的锅状锅中烹饪嫩羊肉块，锅中加入番茄、青辣椒、大蒜、生姜和各种香料。这道菜以其简单快捷的制作而闻名，使用了新鲜的食材来增强味道。Lamb Karahi经常与naan或米饭一起享用，其大胆可口的口味使其成为深受喜爱的食物，展示了巴基斯坦充满活力和多样性的烹饪传统。

Lamb Trotters Stew

Lamb Trotters Stew, known as Paye in Pakistan, is a traditional dish often enjoyed for breakfast. Renowned for its rich and hearty flavors, this dish is made by slow-cooking lamb or goat trotters with aromatic spices like ginger, garlic, and garam masala until they release a gelatinous, flavorful broth. To make Trotters Stew, the trotters are cleaned thoroughly, then simmered for several hours with onions, tomatoes, and spices. The result is a thick, savory stew that is typically served with “naan or roti”(made from whole wheat flour and cooked on a griddle). Its warming properties and nutritious benefits make Trotters Stew a beloved and iconic breakfast dish in Pakistan.

羊蹄炖汤，在巴基斯坦被称为Paye，是一道经常作为早餐享用的传统菜肴。这道菜以其浓郁的味道而闻名，它是用生姜、大蒜和加兰马萨拉等芳香香料慢煮羊蹄或羊蹄，直到它们释放出凝胶状的美味肉汤。要做猪蹄炖菜，猪蹄要彻底清洗，然后用洋葱、西红柿和香料炖几个小时。其结果是一道浓稠可口的炖菜，通常与“naan或roti”（由全麦面粉制成，在煎锅上烹饪）一起食用。羊蹄炖汤的温暖特性和营养益处使其成为巴基斯坦备受喜爱的标志性早餐菜肴。
