About Me

Welcome to My Life in China

Hello! I’m Rana Arman, a 23-year-old student from Pakistan, currently pursuing my studies in China. Living in this vibrant country has been an incredible journey, filled with enriching experiences and cultural discoveries. My passion for sports has always been a big part of my life. Whether it's playing football, cricket, or badminton, I love the thrill of competition and the camaraderie it brings.

In addition to sports, I enjoy exploring new places, meeting diverse people, and immersing myself in the local traditions and lifestyle. Through this website, I aim to share my adventures, insights, and the unique aspects of life in China that I’ve encountered over the past few months. Join me as I navigate through this fascinating journey and discover the many wonders of China.

你好，我叫杨星（Rana Arman），23岁，来自巴基斯坦，目前正在中国学习。生活在这个充满活力的国家是一段令人难以置信的旅程，充满了丰富的经历和文化发现。我对体育的热情一直是我生活中的重要组成部分。无论是踢足球、板球还是羽毛球，我都喜欢比赛带来的刺激和友谊。

除了运动，我还喜欢探索新的地方，结识不同的人，沉浸在当地的传统和生活方式中。通过这个网站，我的目标是分享我在过去几个月里在中国遇到的冒险、见解和生活的独特方面。和我一起走过这段迷人的旅程，发现中国的许多奇迹。
