Anissa (安妮)

Anissa

Anissa is a classmate from Morocco. Although we are not close friends, we always exchange friendly greetings and polite conversations. She brings a unique cultural perspective to our class, and it's interesting to hear about her experiences and traditions. She is dedicated to her studies and consistently performs well in our courses. We occasionally work on group projects together, where she demonstrates a strong work ethic and valuable insights. Even though we don't interact much outside of class, I respect her commitment and the diversity she brings to our classroom environment. It’s always nice to see her around campus, and our brief exchanges are always friendly. I'm glad to have her as a part of our class.
